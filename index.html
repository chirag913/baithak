Questions

Before you ask.

What is Baithak Club?

A curated guestlist for small social nights, starting in Gaur City. Not a café, not a club with a cover charge at the door — a room we choose people for.

Who can apply?

Ages 18–35. Every application is read by a person before anyone is invited. Applications from outside that age range are declined.

How does verification work?

We read every application by hand. We look for a real person and a good fit for a mixed, respectful room — not a CV.

Is a seat guaranteed if I apply?

No. Spots are limited and the list is curated by hand.

Can I come alone?

Yes. Most people do.

Where is Bonfire Night?

Gaur City, Greater Noida West. The exact meeting point goes only to invited guests, the night before.

When will I hear back?

If you’re on the list, we’ll WhatsApp you before Saturday. If not, we’ll close the loop. Questions before then? WhatsApp us on +91 99115 90519.

Is there a fee?

Bonfire Night is ₹999 per guest. You only pay if you’re invited — payment details come with your invite. Applying is free.
